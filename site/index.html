How this works. Each week a program searches CourtListener for new published federal appellate opinions that use circuit-split language, reads the relevant passages, and records the question and the circuits on each side. Entries are generated by software and may contain errors. An editor may check or correct an entry; those are marked “Editor-checked.”

This is a research starting point, not legal advice. Read the opinions and confirm that a split still exists before you rely on it.
